# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free learning community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good intent, focus criticism on ideas and code, respect different experience levels, and protect private or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that violate this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented, and protect reporters' privacy.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
